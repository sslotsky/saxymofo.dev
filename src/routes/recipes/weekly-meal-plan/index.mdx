## A daily plan for the week

- **Breakfast:** [Sourdough, cashew butter, and yogurt](/recipes/breakfast-plate/). Toast a large slice of sourdough, spread on <Q>2 tbsp</Q> cashew butter, and serve with <Q>3/4 cup</Q> lactose-free Greek yogurt mixed with <Q>2 tbsp</Q> hemp hearts. [Fruit syrup](/recipes/fruit-syrup/) is optional.
- **Lunch:** [Big Crunch Hummus & Grain Bowl](/recipes/big-crunch-bowl/): tender brown rice, homemade hummus, roasted pumpkin and sunflower seeds, and cucumbers or carrots with tamari and sesame oil.
- **Afternoon:** [Crunch snack](/recipes/crunch-snack/) of roasted nuts or crunchy edamame. Alternatively, dip crisp cucumbers or sourdough crackers into hummus.
- **Dinner:** [Coconut-Cashew Rice Bowl](/recipes/coconut-cashew-rice-bowl/) with rice or quinoa, crisp-tender broccoli or whole peas, and roasted cashews or sliced almonds.

## Prepare the components

Follow the weekend schedule for [toaster sourdough](/recipes/toaster-sourdough/), then freeze the cooled slices for toast. Make [roasted cashew butter](/recipes/roasted-cashew-butter/) for breakfasts and [dinner sauce](/recipes/coconut-cashew-sauce/).

Remix the tahini when opening a new jar, then use it for [ultra-smooth hummus](/recipes/ultra-smooth-hummus/) with the Roasted Garlic, Roasted Red Pepper, or Pine Nut Topping variation. Make [fruit syrup](/recipes/fruit-syrup/) if desired.

The shopping list covers the components and their optional variations, not fixed weekly quantities. Keep an active sourdough starter on hand for the Friday feed.
