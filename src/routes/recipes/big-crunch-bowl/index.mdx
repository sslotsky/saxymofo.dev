---
title: Big Crunch Hummus & Grain Bowl
description: Brown rice, smooth hummus, roasted seeds, and finely chopped vegetables.
tags: [meals]
yield: 1 serving
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 1 1/2 cups cooked short-grain brown rice
      - 3 generous tbsp homemade ultra-smooth hummus
      - 3 tbsp shelled dry-roasted pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
      - 3 tbsp roasted sunflower seed kernels
      - 1/2 cup finely diced raw cucumbers or shredded carrots
      - tamari or regular soy sauce, a savory splash
      - 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
---

Cook the brown rice until tender in a rice cooker. Add it to a bowl with [ultra-smooth hummus](/recipes/ultra-smooth-hummus/), the pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, and vegetables.

Dress with a splash of tamari or soy sauce and the toasted sesame oil.
