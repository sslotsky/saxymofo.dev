---
title: Coconut-Cashew Dinner Sauce
description: A quick personal-blender sauce for a warm rice or quinoa bowl.
tags: [sauces]
yield: 1 serving
time: 1 min
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 1/4 cup smooth homemade roasted cashew butter
      - 1/3 cup canned full-fat coconut milk
      - 1 tbsp tamari or regular soy sauce
      - 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
      - 1 tsp fresh lime juice
---

Shake the coconut milk can hard before opening. Add all ingredients to the Ninja CrushBOSS 26-oz BlendBOSS tumbler, screw on the blade assembly, dock it on the CrushBOSS base, and run **Custom → High** for 20–30 seconds until completely uniform.

Pour it over a hot [coconut-cashew rice bowl](/recipes/coconut-cashew-rice-bowl/). Made with [roasted cashew butter](/recipes/roasted-cashew-butter/).
