## Key Adjustments for Your Setup

* **Scaled to 375 g Total Flour:** Sized specifically for an 8.5" × 4.5" pan (~820 g total dough weight with inclusions) so the oven spring stays neatly inside the inverted top-pan dome.
* **Hydration & Flour:** Uses 20% white whole wheat (<Q>75 g</Q>) and <Q>300 g</Q> bread flour with <Q>260 g</Q> water and <Q>15 g</Q> orange juice (~73% liquid, or ~75% effective hydration with the honey and fresh cranberries).
* **Honey Incorporation:** Whisk the warmed honey directly into the warm water, orange juice, and starter during the initial mix so it dissolves completely without tearing early gluten strands.
* **Coil-Fold Inclusions:** Fold the chopped cranberries and zest in during your second coil fold so they stay suspended without waterlogging the dough.

## Equipment

An 8.5" × 4.5" aluminized steel loaf pan (such as a USA Pan), a second matching loaf pan to invert as a steam dome, a bench scraper, a bread lame, and parchment paper.

## Mix & Bulk Ferment

1. **Dissolve & Mix:** Warm <Q>15 g</Q> honey for 5–10 seconds until runny. In your mixing bowl, whisk the warm honey, <Q>260 g</Q> warm water, <Q>15 g</Q> fresh orange juice, and <Q>75 g</Q> peaked starter until milky.
2. **Add Flour & Salt:** Add <Q>300 g</Q> bread flour, <Q>75 g</Q> white whole wheat flour, and <Q>8 g</Q> fine sea salt. Mix by hand until no dry flour remains. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
3. **Coil Fold 1:** With damp hands, perform your first set of 4 gentle coil folds (lifting from the center and tucking the ends underneath, rotating the bowl 90° each time). Cover and rest 30 minutes.
4. **Coil Fold 2 (Add Inclusions):** Stretch the dough gently into a rectangle in the bowl or on a damp counter. Scatter two-thirds of the <Q>75 g</Q> roughly chopped fresh cranberries and <Q>orange zest</Q> over the surface, fold into thirds like a letter, scatter the remaining third on top, and tuck into a ball. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
5. **Coil Folds 3 & 4:** Perform 1 to 2 more gentle coil folds spaced 30 minutes apart until the dough feels airy, cohesive, and holds a dome without cranberries poking through the surface.
6. **Finish Bulk Rise:** Cover and let finish bulk fermenting at room temperature until risen by about 50–75% with visible bubbles along the sides and bottom (roughly 4 to 6 hours total from mixing; note that honey slightly accelerates fermentation).

## Shape & Cold Proof

1. **Pre-Shape & Rest:** Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured counter using your bench scraper. Gently shape into a loose round, cover, and bench-rest for 15 minutes.
2. **Final Log Shape:** Lightly dust the top with flour, flip seam-side up, and gently pat into an 8-inch wide rectangle. Fold the left and right edges inward to match the 8.5-inch pan length, then roll tightly from the bottom edge up into a taut cylinder, pinching the seam closed.
3. **Load Pan & Retard:** Transfer seam-side down into a parchment-lined (or greased) 8.5" × 4.5" loaf pan. Cover tightly and refrigerate for **12 to 16 hours**.

## Bake (Double-Pan Method)

1. **Preheat:** Place a baking sheet or stone on the center rack and preheat the oven to **450°F (230°C)** for 30 minutes.
2. **Score & Cover:** Take the cold loaf straight from the fridge, score a single ½-inch deep slash down the center with a bread lame, and invert a second 8.5" × 4.5" loaf pan over the top to trap steam.
3. **Covered Bake:** Bake covered at **450°F** for **25 minutes**.
4. **Uncovered Bake:** Carefully remove the top pan with oven mitts, drop the temperature to **425°F (220°C)**, and bake uncovered for **20 to 25 minutes** until deep golden brown (internal temp **205–210°F**). Because honey browns faster than plain dough, tent loosely with foil for the last 5–10 minutes if the crust darkens too quickly.
5. **Cool Completely:** Transfer to a wire rack and wait at least **2 hours** before slicing so the warm cranberry juices set without making the crumb gummy.
