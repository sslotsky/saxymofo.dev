      - 345 g warm water (80–85°F)
      - 100 g active sourdough starter (100% hydration)
      - 20 g fresh orange juice (from the zested orange)
      - 20 g warm honey
      - 10 g fine sea salt
  - group: Inclusions
    items:
      - 100 g fresh cranberries, roughly chopped
      - Zest of 1 large orange

## Key Adjustments for Your Setup

* **Hydration & Flour:** Swapped 20% of the bread flour for white whole wheat and bumped the water from 320 g to <Q>345 g</Q> (bringing total liquid with the orange juice to 73%, or ~75% effective hydration with the honey and juicy fresh cranberries).
* **Honey Incorporation:** Whisk the warmed honey directly into the warm water, orange juice, and starter during the initial mix rather than kneading it in later—it dissolves completely without tearing early gluten strands.
* **Lamination / Coil-Fold Inclusions:** Fresh chopped cranberries are wet and heavy, so folding them in during your second coil fold (instead of all at once during mixing) keeps them evenly suspended without waterlogging the dough.
* **Loaf Pan & Double-Pan Bake:** Shaped as a tight log into an 8.5" × 4.5" loaf pan and baked using an inverted second loaf pan (or foil tent) for steam.

## Equipment

An 8.5" × 4.5" aluminized steel loaf pan (such as a USA Pan), a second matching loaf pan (or heavy-duty foil) to invert as a steam dome, a bench scraper, a bread lame, and parchment paper.

## Mix & Bulk Ferment

1. **Dissolve & Mix:** Warm <Q>20 g</Q> honey for 5–10 seconds until runny. In your mixing bowl, whisk the warm honey, <Q>345 g</Q> warm water, <Q>20 g</Q> fresh orange juice, and <Q>100 g</Q> peaked starter until milky.
2. **Add Flour & Salt:** Add <Q>400 g</Q> bread flour, <Q>100 g</Q> white whole wheat flour, and <Q>10 g</Q> fine sea salt. Mix by hand until no dry flour remains. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
3. **Coil Fold 1:** With damp hands, perform your first set of 4 gentle coil folds (lifting from the center and tucking the ends underneath, rotating the bowl 90° each time). Cover and rest 30 minutes.
4. **Coil Fold 2 (Add Inclusions):** Stretch the dough gently into a rectangle in the bowl or on a damp counter. Scatter two-thirds of the <Q>100 g</Q> roughly chopped fresh cranberries and <Q>orange zest</Q> over the surface, fold into thirds like a letter, scatter the remaining third on top, and roll/tuck into a ball. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
5. **Coil Folds 3 & 4:** Perform 1 to 2 more gentle coil folds spaced 30 minutes apart until the dough feels airy, cohesive, and holds a dome without cranberries poking through the surface.
6. **Finish Bulk Rise:** Cover and let finish bulk fermenting at room temperature until risen by about 50–75% with visible bubbles along the sides and bottom (roughly 4 to 6 hours total from mixing, depending on kitchen temp; note that honey slightly accelerates fermentation).

## Shape & Cold Proof

1. **Pre-Shape & Rest:** Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured counter using your bench scraper. Gently shape into a loose round, cover, and bench-rest for 15 minutes.
2. **Final Log Shape:** Lightly dust the top with flour, flip seam-side up, and gently pat into an 8-inch wide rectangle. Fold the left and right edges inward to match the 8.5-inch pan length, then roll tightly from the bottom edge up into a taut cylinder, pinching the seam closed.
3. **Load Pan & Retard:** Transfer seam-side down into a parchment-lined (or greased) 8.5" × 4.5" loaf pan. Cover tightly with a shower cap or plastic wrap and refrigerate for **12 to 16 hours**.

## Bake (Double-Pan Method)

1. **Preheat:** Place a baking sheet or stone on the center rack and preheat the oven to **450°F (230°C)** for 30 minutes. *(If using a cold-start oven, place the covered pan in before turning the oven on and add 10 minutes to the covered bake time.)*
2. **Score & Cover:** Take the cold loaf straight from the fridge, score a single ½-inch deep slash down the center with a bread lame, and invert a second 8.5" × 4.5" loaf pan over the top to trap steam.
3. **Covered Bake:** Bake covered at **450°F** for **25 minutes**.
4. **Uncovered Bake:** Carefully remove the top pan with oven mitts, drop the temperature to **425°F (220°C)**, and bake uncovered for **20 to 25 minutes** until deep golden brown (internal temp **205–210°F**). Because honey browns faster than plain dough, tent loosely with foil for the last 5–10 minutes if the crust darkens too quickly.
5. **Cool Completely:** Transfer to a wire rack and wait at least **2 hours** before slicing—cutting early lets the warm cranberry juices make the crumb gummy.
