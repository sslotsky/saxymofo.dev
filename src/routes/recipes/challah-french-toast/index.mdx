## Method

1. **Whisk the batter.** Pour <Q>1/2 cup</Q> Milkadamia into a measuring cup, then add avocado oil up to the <Q>2/3 cup</Q> line. Add the eggs, brown sugar, salt, and optional cinnamon, then whisk together.
2. **Heat the skillet.** Heat a cast-iron skillet a little above medium until it's a bit too hot. Splash it with a few tablespoons of water to cool it down, then turn the heat to a bit less than medium.
3. **Fry the toast.** Melt just enough butter to cover the pan. Dip the slices in the batter on both sides, then fry until deeply browned on both sides, adding butter between batches as needed.
4. **Keep warm.** Hold the finished toast on a sheet pan in a 200°F (95°C) oven until someone's ready to grab it.

Cast iron is the usual pan, but a HexClad-style hybrid pan works fine too. The photo was made in one.
