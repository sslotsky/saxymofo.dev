---
title: Coconut-Cashew Rice Bowl
description: Warm rice or quinoa with a creamy coconut-cashew sauce, vegetables, and roasted nuts.
tags: [meals]
yield: 1 serving
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 1 1/2 cups cooked short-grain brown rice or quinoa
      - 1 batch coconut-cashew dinner sauce
      - 1/2 cup crisp-tender roasted broccoli florets or whole green peas
      - roasted cashew halves or sliced almonds, a handful
---

Cook the rice or quinoa in a rice cooker and keep warm. Blend [coconut-cashew dinner sauce](/recipes/coconut-cashew-sauce/) until completely uniform and pour it over the hot grains.

Add the broccoli or peas. Toss the roasted cashew halves or sliced almonds over the top just before eating.
