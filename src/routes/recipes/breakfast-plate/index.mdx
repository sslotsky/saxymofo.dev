---
title: Sourdough, Cashew Butter & Yogurt Breakfast Plate
description: Crunchy toast with cashew butter and a side of yogurt mixed with hemp hearts.
tags: [meals]
yield: 1 serving
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 1 large slice homemade sourdough bread
      - 2 generous tbsp smooth roasted cashew butter
      - 3/4 cup plain lactose-free Greek yogurt
      - 2 tbsp raw hemp hearts
      - fruit syrup, optional
---

Toast the [sourdough](/recipes/toaster-sourdough/) until extra crunchy and spread with [roasted cashew butter](/recipes/roasted-cashew-butter/).

Stir the hemp hearts completely into the yogurt and serve alongside. Add a little [fruit syrup](/recipes/fruit-syrup/) if desired.
