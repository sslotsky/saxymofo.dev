Use <Q>1/2 cup</Q> water for a thick dip or sour-cream consistency, or up to <Q>3/4 cup</Q> for pourable cream or pasta sauce. This works in a standard personal blender without straining.

## Method

1. **Soak.** Put the cashew pieces in a heatproof bowl and cover with boiling water. Soak for 15–20 minutes, then drain and rinse.
2. **Blend.** Blend the soaked cashews with <Q>1/2 cup</Q> fresh water and the remaining cream ingredients for your selected variation. Blend on high for 60–90 seconds, scraping down once. Add fresh water <Q>1 tbsp</Q> at a time until it reaches the consistency you want.

<Variation name="base">

The plain cream uses lemon juice (or apple cider vinegar) and fine sea salt.

</Variation>

<Variation name="Nacho Cheese">

Blend in the nutritional yeast, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, and turmeric for a dairy-free nacho or cheese sauce.

</Variation>

<Variation name="Garlic Herb Pasta Sauce">

For the roasted garlic, follow the roasting step in [roasted garlic hummus](/recipes/ultra-smooth-hummus/?v=roasted-garlic). Blend the roasted garlic, nutritional yeast, and black pepper into the cream along with the lemon juice and salt. Toss directly with hot pasta and a splash of pasta water.

</Variation>

<Variation name="Sweet Vanilla Cream">

Leave out the lemon juice and original salt. Blend in the maple syrup, vanilla, and small pinch of salt instead. Serve with fruit or [challah french toast](/recipes/challah-french-toast/).

</Variation>
