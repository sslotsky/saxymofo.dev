## Method

1. **Roast.** Roast <Q>1 lb</Q> raw whole cashews (about <Q>3 cups</Q>) at 325°F (165°C) for 10–14 minutes, until golden.
2. **Season while hot.** Immediately after taking the cashews out of the oven, toss them with the binder ingredients for your selected flavor, then the seasoning. Adding the spices after roasting helps them stick without burning.

<Variation name="base">

For Sweet & Spicy Maple, toss the hot cashews with maple syrup and melted coconut or neutral oil, then the salt, cayenne, and cinnamon.

</Variation>

<Variation name="Smoky BBQ / Cajun">

Toss the hot cashews with neutral oil, then the salt, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and cayenne.

</Variation>

<Variation name="Rosemary Sea Salt">

Toss the hot cashews with olive oil, then the minced rosemary, salt, and black pepper.

</Variation>

<Variation name="Honey Mustard">

Toss the hot cashews with honey and melted butter or neutral oil, then the mustard powder, salt, onion powder, and turmeric.

</Variation>

<Variation name="Chili Lime">

Toss the hot cashews with neutral oil and lime zest, then the salt, chili powder, and cumin. Squeeze fresh lime juice over them right before serving.

</Variation>

## A note on salt

Coarse salt crystals bounce off smooth nuts, so use fine sea salt, or pulse kosher salt in a spice grinder for 2 seconds so it sticks evenly. For the Rosemary Sea Salt variation, the seasoning list also allows flaky salt.
