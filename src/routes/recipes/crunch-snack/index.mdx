---
title: Crunch Snack
description: Roasted nuts or crisp edamame, with an easy hummus-and-cucumber alternative.
tags: [meals]
yield: 1 serving
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 1/4 cup whole roasted almonds, whole roasted cashews, or crispy dry-roasted edamame
---

Serve the nuts or edamame in a small bowl for an afternoon snack.

For a change, dip crisp cucumber slices or crunchy sourdough crackers into [ultra-smooth hummus](/recipes/ultra-smooth-hummus/). Use [homemade sourdough](/recipes/toaster-sourdough/) for the bread component of the week.
