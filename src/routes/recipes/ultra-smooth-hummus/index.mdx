Use a Nutribullet Ultra and follow this loading order to prevent air pockets. Blend in batches if scaling beyond the cup's capacity.

## Before you start: remix the tahini

When opening a new 16 oz jar of tahini, pour all the floating oil and the solid sesame paste into a full-sized Ninja pitcher. Process on high for 30 seconds until smooth and homogenized. Pour it back into the original jar and store it **upside down in the fridge**, so it stays easy to spoon out rather than separating into a hard block. Scaling the recipe doesn't change this step.

<Variation name="Roasted Garlic">

## Roast the garlic

Omit the raw garlic. Wrap a whole head of garlic in foil and roast at 350°F (175°C) for 40 minutes. Squeeze <Q>4</Q> soft, caramelized cloves into the cream base before blending.

</Variation>

## Loading order

1. **Liquid first:** Add <Q>3 tbsp</Q> fresh lemon juice, <Q>2 tbsp</Q> extra-virgin olive oil, <Q>3 tbsp</Q> remixed tahini, the garlic, <Q>1/2 tsp</Q> fine sea salt, and <Q>1/4 tsp</Q> ground cumin to the cup. Blend on high for 15 seconds into a smooth cream.
2. **Chickpeas:** Pour <Q>1 (15 oz) can</Q> thoroughly rinsed and drained chickpeas on top. Pulse 5 times, then blend on high for 30 seconds.
3. **Ice trick:** Stop the blender, open the cup, and pour <Q>2 to 3 tbsp</Q> ice-cold water directly onto the blades. Blend for a final 20 seconds. The cold water fluffs the tahini into a lighter cream.

<Variation name="Roasted Red Pepper">

Pat the roasted red pepper completely dry with a paper towel and add it to the cream base in step 1 before blending.

</Variation>

<Variation name="Pine Nut Topping">

Keep the hummus plain and scatter <Q>2 tbsp</Q> pan-toasted pine nuts on top of the bowl rather than blending them in.

</Variation>
