---
title: Roasted Cashew Butter
description: Roast the cashews, then blend them into a smooth butter in a Nutribullet Ultra cup.
tags: [spreads]
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 3 cups (about 400 g) raw whole cashews
      - 1 tbsp neutral oil (avocado or grapeseed)
      - 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
---

## Equipment

A Nutribullet Ultra 1200W personal blender, a 24-oz or 32-oz Tritan single-serve cup with a stay-fresh twist lid, and a bare, rimmed baking sheet. Work in batches when scaling; don't exceed the cup's fill limit.

## Roast

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Spread <Q>3 cups</Q> raw cashews in a flat, single layer on the baking sheet.
2. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, shaking the sheet firmly at the 6-minute mark. Watch closely during the final 3 minutes: cashews go from golden to scorched quickly. Remove them as soon as they turn light golden brown and smell nutty.
3. Rest on the counter until warm to the touch, roughly 5 to 10 minutes. Do not blend boiling-hot nuts straight from the oven: trapped steam can damage the blade seals. Warm cashews release their fats faster than cold ones.

## Blend

1. Add the warm cashews, <Q>1/2 tsp</Q> sea salt, and <Q>1 tbsp</Q> neutral oil directly to the cup.
2. Secure the extractor blade tightly and dock the cup in the motor base. Apply 5 to 7 sharp, short pulses to break the nuts into a coarse meal without binding the lower blades.
3. Run a continuous high-speed blend for 45 to 60 seconds. The cup's vortex pulls the paste back through the blades.
4. If meal sticks at the top rim, stop the base, give the cup a firm shake or tap on the counter, and blend for another 15 seconds until glossy and smooth. Adjust the salt at the end.

## Storage and cleanup

Remove the blade assembly and screw the storage lid onto the cup. Refrigerate; the butter thickens as it chills and keeps for up to 1 full month.

Rinse the extractor blade under hot running water for 5 to 10 seconds as soon as it comes off the cup, before the butter dries or hardens under the gaskets.
