---
title: Roasted Cashew Butter
description: Roast the cashews, then process them into a smooth butter in the Ninja CrushBOSS food processor bowl.
tags: [spreads]
ingredients:
  - items:
      - 3 cups (about 400 g) raw whole cashews
      - 1 tbsp neutral oil (avocado or grapeseed)
      - 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
---

## Equipment

A Ninja CrushBOSS 3-in-1 Kitchen System fitted with the 64-oz (8-cup) TotalPrepBOSS Food Processor Bowl, chopping blade assembly, and feed-chute lid, plus a silicone spatula, a glass storage jar, and a bare, rimmed baking sheet.

## Roast

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Spread <Q>3 cups</Q> raw cashews in a flat, single layer on the baking sheet.
2. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, shaking the sheet firmly at the 6-minute mark. Watch closely during the final 3 minutes: cashews go from golden to scorched quickly. Remove them as soon as they turn light golden brown and smell nutty.
3. Rest on the counter until warm to the touch, roughly 5 to 10 minutes. Warm cashews release their fats faster than cold ones without trapping excess steam inside the sealed processor bowl.

## Process

1. Seat the 64-oz food processor bowl on the CrushBOSS motor base and place the chopping blade assembly on the center spindle. Add the warm cashews and <Q>1/2 tsp</Q> sea salt, holding the oil back until the nuts break down so the blades grip cleanly. Lock the feed-chute lid in place.
2. Turn the dial to **Custom**, select **Low**, and run for 60 to 90 seconds until the whole cashews break down into a fine, crumbly meal.
3. Switch to **Medium** (or **High** once the meal starts clumping) and run for 2 to 3 minutes. The meal will gather into a thick ball before smoothing out into a paste. Stop once midway through to scrape down the bowl walls with a silicone spatula.
4. Drop back to **Low**, remove the feed-chute pusher, and slowly drizzle <Q>1 tbsp</Q> neutral oil through the chute while the motor runs.
5. Return to **High** for 30 to 60 seconds until glossy and fluid. Taste and adjust the salt at the end.

## Storage and cleanup

Transfer the cashew butter to a clean, airtight glass jar. Refrigerate; the butter thickens as it chills and keeps for up to 1 full month.

Lift out the chopping blade assembly and rinse both the blade and processor bowl under hot running water right away, before the butter cools and sets on the plastic.
